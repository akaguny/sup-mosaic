# SUP Mosaic

A stencil generator for fuse beads (thermal mosaic) on SUP boards: full-size board models and keychains. You put boards on the peg field, the app counts beads by colour, draws bead cells in the free space of the sheet and saves a 1:1 PDF for printing.

App: https://akaguny.github.io/sup-mosaic/
